## Walls and decor {#walls}

The golden rule: **removable adhesive over anything permanent.**

1. **Peel-and-stick wallpaper** transforms one accent wall and peels off cleanly.
2. **Removable adhesive hooks** hold art, coats, and keys — check the weight rating before you hang.
3. **Damage-free picture-hanging strips** let you build a gallery wall with zero nail holes.
4. **Tension rods** create room dividers, extra hanging space, or plant displays with no hardware.
5. **Washi tape** frames posters or outlines a faux headboard for a few dollars.

## Lighting {#light}

Rental lighting is usually an afterthought. Fix it without an electrician:

6. **Plug-in pendant lights** hang from an adhesive ceiling hook and plug into the wall.
7. **Rechargeable, stick-on puck lights** brighten dark closets and under-cabinet zones.
8. **Smart bulbs** add dimming and warm tones to any existing fixture.
9. **Battery-powered motion sensor lights** for hallways and entryways.
10. **A warm-toned floor lamp** does more for a room's mood than the builder-grade ceiling light ever will.

## Storage without holes {#storage}

11. **Over-the-door organizers** for shoes, pantry items, or bathroom supplies.
12. **Tension-rod shelving** inside cabinets and closets.
13. **Freestanding shelving units** that never touch the wall.
14. **Under-bed storage bins** to reclaim the biggest wasted space in the home.
15. **Stackable, modular cube storage** that reconfigures with you at the next place.

## Kitchen and bath {#kitchen}

16. **Adhesive backsplash tiles** cover a dated kitchen wall and peel off later.
17. **A magnetic knife strip** and **over-the-cabinet-door racks** (no drilling).
18. **A handheld or clip-on sink sprayer** to upgrade a weak faucet.
19. **Peel-and-stick vinyl** to reface an ugly countertop temporarily.
20. **Suction or adhesive bathroom caddies** instead of drilled-in shelves.

## Floors {#floors}

21. **Peel-and-stick vinyl floor tiles** cover cracked or dated flooring and lift off later.
22. **Large area rugs** to hide bad floors and warm up cold ones.
23. **Interlocking foam or cork tiles** for a home gym or kids' corner.
