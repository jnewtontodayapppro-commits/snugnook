The problem with a tiny kitchen is almost never storage capacity — it's *access*. Counters vanish under appliances you use once a week, cabinets swallow things you can't reach, and the walls sit empty. Here's how to fix all three, starting today and mostly for free.

## Clear the counters first {#counters}

Counter space is the most valuable real estate you own, so nothing lives there unless you use it daily. Everything else — the stand mixer, the blender, the air fryer you love but use twice a week — moves to a cabinet or shelf. The single move that frees the most space: mount a **magnetic knife strip** and a **paper-towel holder** on the wall, and get your dish rack off the counter with an **over-the-sink roll-up drying rack** that stows away when dry.

## Go vertical and use the walls {#vertical}

Empty wall is unused storage. A few renter-friendly options:

- A **pegboard** turns a bare wall into fully customizable hanging storage for pots, utensils, and mugs.
- **Rail systems** with S-hooks keep utensils and cups within reach but off the counter.
- A **narrow rolling cart** slides into the gap beside the fridge or counter and pulls out three tiers of storage from a 6-inch-wide slot.
- **Floating shelves** above the counter hold everyday dishes and free up a whole cabinet.

## Mine the cabinet doors {#doors}

The inside of every cabinet door is prime, forgotten storage. Add-on **over-the-door racks** and adhesive bins hold pot lids, cutting boards, foil and wrap, and cleaning supplies — often doubling a cabinet's usable capacity without touching a shelf.

## Fix the cabinet black holes {#cabinets}

Deep cabinets waste their back half because you can't reach it. Two cheap fixes solve most of it:

- **Pull-out drawers / sliding bins** bring the back of the cabinet to you.
- **Shelf risers** double a shelf's height so plates and cans stack in usable layers instead of one deep, unreachable pile.
- A **lazy Susan** in a corner cabinet turns dead space into spin-to-reach storage.

For under-sink chaos, a small two-tier expandable shelf works around the pipes to reclaim the space most people write off entirely.
